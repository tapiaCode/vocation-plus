# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS

## Users
High school graduates ("bachilleres") in Santa Cruz, Bolivia, seeking orientation to discover their ideal career path.

## Product Purpose
To centralize and provide verified, comprehensive information about higher education options in Santa Cruz, empowering students to make informed decisions about their academic future.

## Positioning
A platform created *by* and *for* students (as a BTH graduation project) that offers a friction-free 20-question vocational test without registration, along with detailed guides for local universities.

## Operating Context
Students exploring university options, taking vocational tests, researching admission requirements (PSA/PAB), and seeking scholarship information.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Vocational Test (20 questions, no registration required).
- Academic offering catalog of 6+ major universities (UAGRM, UPSA, UCB, UTEPSA, UPDS, UCEBOL).
- Admission and scholarship guides.
- Developed as a High School Technical Humanistic (BTH) graduation project.
- No existing brand commitments; the design, colors, and brand are to be redefined.

## Evidence on Hand
- Team: Elias Montero Montero and Juana Maria Nova Nova.
- Real content, university data, and requirements context is present in the static files.

## Product Principles
1. **Frictionless Access**: Information and tools like the vocational test must be accessible without mandatory registration.
2. **Local Relevance**: Focus strictly on the academic landscape and realities of Santa Cruz, Bolivia.
3. **Empathy**: Designed with the understanding of what a high school senior actually needs and feels during this transition.
